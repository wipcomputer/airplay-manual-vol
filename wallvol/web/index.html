    How to use the panel

    Knob and screen

    • Turn: click and drag a knob in a circle, scroll on it, or press ↑ ↓. Each step is 2%.
    • Wake: click the knob or touch the screen. It dims to 5% after a few seconds.
    • Set a level: tap or slide on the bar on the screen.
    • Mute: double-click the knob, or click the circle on the screen (M).
    • Several knobs: the screen follows the knob you touch and says KNOB 1, 2 or 3 (1 2 3).

    Menu

    • Three clicks, from anywhere: the MENU, with Devices and Settings.
    • In a menu: turn to move, one click picks, two clicks go back (like an iPod's MENU button). Lists end with ‹ Back or Exit.
    • Devices: switch this knob's device, Master Volume (with 2+ speakers), or + Add device.
    • Settings: Brightness, Dim Brightness, Temperature °F / °C, Name, Auto Update, Lock panel.
    • Shortcuts: tap the device's name for every device of that kind; double-tap a temperature for °F / °C.

    Sauna

    • Turn to set the target. While it's off, only the target changes.
    • Hold the round timer or the knob to start or stop it: after 1 s a white ring fills.
    • While it runs, the ring empties over the session and shows the time left.

    Lock

    • Hold the knob (or L): after 3 s a red ring fills, then the panel locks. Not on a sauna: use Settings → Lock panel.
    • First lock: choose a 4-digit code on the panel (SET CODE, then CONFIRM CODE).
    • Unlock: touch or turn, then enter the code (turn and push, tap, or type it), or use Tap iPhone (NFC).

    Setup

    • A new panel shows a code. Click it to set it up the way a buyer would, or scan it with your iPhone when the server runs with --lan.
    • On a setup code, click a knob to set up that knob.
    • + Add panel simulates another panel in another room. Devices sync to every panel; each knob keeps its own device.
